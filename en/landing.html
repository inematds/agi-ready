The Super-Agents They arrived

How your work changes when AI stops waiting for instructions. In 6 lessons, you’ll decide what to hand off to an agent, what it can decide on its own, and who is accountable for it. No code, at your own pace, right in your browser.

~20 min per lesson · nothing to install · works on your phone and computer

What you’ll know how to do

Results, not theory

Separate tasks from responsibilities. Look at your list of requests to AI and point out what’s a recipe and what an agent can handle on its own.

Write a real delegation. Goal, context, limits, approval point, and criteria for good work, tested in an AI chat.

Decide the trust levels. Which decisions the agent handles on its own, which it reports on, which it only handles with you, and who is responsible for each line.

Sign the delegation letter. The six questions answered and one rehearsal completed before handing over anything real.

Who it’s for

Made for people who make decisions, not people who program

is for you if

  • You're an HR manager, owner of a small service business, marketing coordinator, or customer service manager, and you already use an AI chat in your daily work.
  • You heard that “AGI has arrived” and want to know what that changes at your desk, not in the labs.
  • You have little time, no technical background, and would rather leave with something written and tested.

this isn’t for you if

  • You want to build agents with code, integrations, and technical automations.
  • You're looking for a review of GPT-6 Astra or a comparison of models.
  • You want a course with traditional video lessons.
